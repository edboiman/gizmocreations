A happy little start

  1. Set your stitch and row counts, choose colors and describe a supported motif.
  2. Generate a chart, then tap squares to adjust it. Upload artwork for a custom silhouette.
  3. Save your chart under a yarn weight. Add notes and track your current row.
  4. Download a numbered PNG or use Print / PDF. Back up your library regularly.

Before you cast on

Swatch first: stitch shape and gauge change the finished picture. Knit proportions is a preview, not a gauge calculation. This is a color chart, not a complete knitting pattern. Long color runs may need float management; check tension and construction yourself.
