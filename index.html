Monthly price review

How to keep prices current each month
  1. Check your usual shop listings or recent receipts for the yarns you buy. Use the regular price per skein and its label yardage, in USD. Convert meters to yards by multiplying by 1.0936.
  2. Use the form below for a yarn quote or a weight estimate. Weight estimates change the cost ranges across the app for the chosen tier. Named yarn quotes are stored separately for reference.
  3. For several updates, download your current price CSV, edit it in a spreadsheet, save it as CSV, then import it. Keep the type, weight and tier columns. Change the checked date only for rows you actually reviewed.
  4. After checking the prices you use, tap “Finish this month's review.” A new review becomes due on the first day of the next month, when you next open the app.
  5. Export a backup after updating, especially if you use another device.

This is an in-app reminder when you open the app; it does not send a notification while closed. Checking a shop website needs internet. Entering prices, importing a saved CSV and using saved estimates work offline. Finishing a review records your check; it does not fetch or change any prices.

  1. Set your stitch and row counts, pick a motif from the list and choose up to three colors.
  2. Use Knit controls to change the layout, scale, spacing, mirroring and borders.
  3. Upload artwork and tune the sliders, type your own grid, or connect AI for free-form ideas.
  4. Tap squares to adjust. Save under a yarn weight, track your current row, download or print.

Before you cast on

Swatch first: stitch shape and gauge change the finished picture. Knit proportions is a preview, not a gauge calculation. This is a color chart, not a complete knitting pattern. The float check estimates per-color carried gaps, including the row join when knitting in the round. Colors absent from a whole row are not counted; yarn handling still needs a swatch and a human check.

Projects & backups

Use My projects for completed-row counters, repeat positions, needles, gauge, session notes and finishing dates. Start a project from a library card to link its pattern. Chart row markers and project counters are separate. Yarn links do not reserve inventory. Export backup is available on every section; it includes projects and the current chart draft.
